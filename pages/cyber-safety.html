Citizen Protection

Cyber Safety & Scam Awareness Center

Learn how to protect yourself from online scams, fake government websites, OTP frauds, UPI frauds, and cybercrime. Stay vigilant and secure.

🚨 Report Cyber Crime

If you have been a victim of cyber fraud, report it immediately to the national authorities.

Helpline: 1930

National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:
cybercrime.gov.in ↗

Emergency Steps
  • !Block your bank account / UPI / cards immediately.
  • !Call 1930 to report the fraudulent transaction.
  • !Preserve all screenshots, chat logs, and transaction IDs as evidence.

Common Online Scams

OTP Fraud

How the Scam Works

Scammers pose as bank officials, delivery agents, or service providers, convincing you to share a One-Time Password (OTP) sent to your phone to access your account.

Warning Signs
  • !Urgent tone demanding immediate action.
  • !Unsolicited calls asking to "verify" or "unblock" an account.
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Never share your OTP or PIN with anyone over a call.
  • ✓Banks and official authorities will never ask for your OTP.

UPI Payment Scam

How the Scam Works

A fraudster expresses intent to buy an item from you or send you money, but instead sends a "Payment Request". They ask you to enter your UPI PIN to "receive" the money.

Warning Signs
  • !Being asked to enter a UPI PIN to receive money.
  • !Notification on your UPI app says "Pay" instead of "Receive".
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Remember: UPI PIN is ONLY needed to SEND money, never to receive.
  • ✓Decline unknown payment requests in your app.

Fake KYC / Account Block Calls

How the Scam Works

You receive an SMS or call claiming your bank account or SIM card will be blocked in 24 hours unless you update your KYC via an provided link or phone number.

Warning Signs
  • !Threats of immediate blocking or account suspension.
  • !Links in SMS that lead to suspicious non-bank websites.
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Do not click on links in SMS messages regarding KYC.
  • ✓Contact your bank branch directly or use the official banking app.

Fake Job Offers

How the Scam Works

Scammers promise high-paying remote jobs but ask the victim to pay a "registration fee," "security deposit," or "equipment fee" before joining.

Warning Signs
  • !Being asked to pay money to get a job.
  • !Offers that seem too good to be true for minimal work.
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Legitimate employers NEVER ask candidates to pay for a job.
  • ✓Verify the company's official website and contact details independently.

QR Code Scam

How the Scam Works

Fraudsters send you a QR code via WhatsApp or email, claiming that scanning it will deposit money into your account for an online sale or prize.

Warning Signs
  • !Asking you to scan a QR code to *receive* a payment.
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Scanning a QR code ALWAYS deducts money from your account.
  • ✓Never scan a code sent by an unknown person.

Fake Government Websites

How the Scam Works

Scammers create lookalike websites of official portals (like PM-KISAN, Aadhaar, NSP) to steal your identity details or extract fake application fees.

Warning Signs
  • !URLs ending in .com, .org, or .xyz instead of .gov.in.
  • !The site asks for an "application processing fee" via a personal UPI ID.
How to Stay Safe
  • ✓Only use domains ending in .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • ✓Check for the HTTPS padlock in the address bar.

Latest Government Advisories & Updates

✅ Verify Government Websites

Before submitting documents like your Aadhaar or PAN, always ensure the website is an official Government of India portal.

  • ✓Domain Check: Official government domains always end with .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • ✓Secure Connection: Look for https:// and the padlock icon in your browser's address bar.
  • ✓Avoid Random Links: Do not click on links received via WhatsApp, Telegram, or unknown SMS senders.
  • ✓Use Official Portals: Search through verified directories like india.gov.in or myscheme.gov.in.

Safe Digital Practices

Strong Passwords

Use long, unique passwords containing numbers and symbols. Don't use your name or date of birth.

Two-Factor Auth

Enable 2FA on your email, banking apps, and social media to add an extra layer of protection.

Safe UPI Usage

Never share your UPI PIN. Remember it's only required to send money, not to receive.

Safe Doc Sharing

Self-attest photocopies of documents specifying the purpose and date to prevent misuse.
